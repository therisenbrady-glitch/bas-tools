# Phone setup

The tools run in your phone's web browser, so there's nothing to install from an app store. Open them from the Field Tools page:

**https://therisenbrady-glitch.github.io/bas-tools/**

Jobs are saved in the browser on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, so **Export job** is your backup and how you hand a job to someone else.

---

## Android

These steps are for **Chrome**, the default browser on most Android phones. Menus can look slightly different by phone maker and Android version. Samsung Internet differences are noted below.

### Open the tools
1. Open the Field Tools link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the tool you want.

Open the tools from the link rather than tapping a downloaded `.html` file. Opened from Downloads, the device scanner's live camera won't work.

### Add to your home screen
1. With a tool open in Chrome, tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
2. Tap **Add to home screen** (it may say **Install app**), then **Add** / **Install**.
3. The tool gets its own icon and opens full screen.

Do this for the Field Tools page and for each tool you use.

**Samsung Internet:** tap **≡** (bottom right) → **Add page to** → **Home screen**.

### Camera (Device Scanner)
- The first time you tap **Scan code**, Chrome asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow** (or **While using the app**).
- If you tapped **Don't allow**:
  1. Tap the **settings icon** at the left of the address bar.
  2. Tap **Permissions** → **Camera** → **Allow**.
  3. Reload the page.
- Still blocked? Go to Android **Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Camera** and set it to **Allow only while using the app**.
- **Photo / image** works without camera permission. It opens the camera app or your gallery.

### Saving exports
- **Export job**, **Save checkout CSV**, and **Export for checkout** save to your **Downloads** folder. Chrome shows a "Download complete" bar with **Open**.
- Find them later in the **Files** app (or **My Files** on Samsung) under **Downloads**.
- To send one, open Files → Downloads, long-press the file → **Share** → email, Teams, text, etc.

### Importing files
- Tap **Import file** (or **Photo / image** in the scanner). Android's file picker opens.
- Tap **☰** (or **Browse**) to switch between **Downloads**, **Drive**, **OneDrive**, and other places.
- Files sent to you by email or Teams: save them to the phone first, then pick them from Downloads.
- The tracker opens EBO `.xml`, Niagara `.bog`, Excel/CSV, scanner exports, and job files.

### Keeping your jobs safe
- Chrome keeps saved jobs until you clear them. **Settings → Privacy → Delete browsing data** with **Cookies and site data** checked erases them, so export first.
- Incognito tabs don't keep anything after they're closed. Don't use them for real jobs.
- Each browser keeps its own copy. A job started in Chrome isn't in Samsung Internet, and the reverse.

## iPhone

### Open the tools
1. Open the Field Tools link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the tool you want.

Don't open the tools from an email attachment or the Files app preview. The page shows up, but the buttons don't work.

### Add to your Home Screen
1. With a tool open in Safari, tap **Share** (the square with an arrow).
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

Do this for each tool you use. It opens full screen, and it also protects your saved jobs: Safari can clear saved data for websites you haven't visited in about a week, but apps on the Home Screen are kept.

### Camera (Device Scanner)
- The first time you tap **Scan code**, Safari asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow**.
- If you tapped **Don't Allow**: tap **aA** in the address bar → **Website Settings** → **Camera** → **Allow**, then reload.
- Or go to iPhone **Settings → Apps → Safari → Camera** and set it to **Ask** or **Allow**.
- **Photo / image** offers **Take Photo** or **Photo Library**.

### Saving exports
- Exports go to **Files → Downloads** (or wherever Safari's download location is set: **Settings → Apps → Safari → Downloads**).
- Tap the **download arrow** in Safari's address bar to see recent downloads.
- To send one: open it in Files → **Share**.

### Importing files
- Tap **Import file** → **Browse** and pick from **Downloads**, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, etc.
- Files from email: tap the attachment → **Share** → **Save to Files** first, then import.

## Laptop
Open the Field Tools link in **Chrome** or **Edge**. In those, **Export job** and **Save checkout CSV** let you choose the folder and file name. The scanner's **Scan code** uses the laptop's webcam; **Photo / image** reads a picture you've copied over from your phone.
